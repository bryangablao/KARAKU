Summary

Betrayed by the woman he loved and abandoned to die in a frozen apocalypse, Elias Veyron gets a second chance at life when he mysteriously regresses 30 days before the world’s destruction.

Armed with memories of the future and a mysterious system, he awakens Seven Deadly Sins: Gluttony, a terrifying ability that allows him to devour enemies, absorb their energy, and evolve beyond human limits.

As civilization collapses and mutated zombies, powerful monsters, and ruthless survivors emerge, Elias prepares to build his own empire. But the apocalypse hides secrets far greater than humanity’s survival.

In a world where the strong devour the weak, he will rise from the ashes, consume everything in his path, and become the one existence even monsters will fear.

This time, he won’t lose everything. This time, he will devour the world.

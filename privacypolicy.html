Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how KARAKU, operated under Accxs Creations, handles information when you browse the website, use search and theme controls, or interact with embedded or linked video content.

This policy reflects the current KARAKU front-end code. KARAKU currently does not include user accounts, checkout, subscriptions, contact forms, or newsletter registration in the supplied website code.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to the KARAKU website and the information handled when you access or interact with it. It does not control the privacy practices of external websites, video hosts, social platforms, hosting providers, or other third parties.

Third-party services have their own privacy policies, cookie practices, and terms.

2. Information We Handle

Based on the current KARAKU website code, the site itself uses a limited amount of browser-side data. The website may also cause information to be transmitted to third-party providers when external content is loaded or played.

Theme preference

KARAKU stores your selected light or dark theme in your browser using a local storage key named karaku-theme.

Search and browsing interaction

The current search box filters the catalog in the browser. The supplied front-end code does not send your search text to a KARAKU server.

Video interaction

When you load or play third-party videos, the relevant external provider may receive technical and usage information directly from your browser.

3. Personal Information

The current KARAKU front-end does not ask you to create an account or directly submit your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, payment information, or similar account information.

If KARAKU later adds accounts, forms, comments, subscriptions, purchases, analytics, advertising, or other features that collect personal information, this Privacy Policy should be updated before or when those features are introduced.

Information may still be processed indirectly by website hosting infrastructure and third-party content providers, as described below.

4. Local Storage

KARAKU uses browser local storage to remember your theme preference. Local storage is different from a traditional cookie but can also store information in your browser.

The current site stores the value for the key karaku-theme so that your light or dark theme remains selected when you return.

You can remove this information through your browser’s site-data or storage controls. Removing it will normally reset the website to its default theme the next time the site loads.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The current KARAKU front-end code does not set a KARAKU cookie for theme selection. Instead, it uses local storage.

However, third-party embedded content may set or read cookies, local storage, device identifiers, or similar technologies when the content is loaded or when you interact with it. These technologies are controlled by the relevant third-party provider.

Depending on the provider, such technologies may be used for video functionality, security, fraud prevention, remembering preferences, measuring usage, maintaining a signed-in session, recommendations, or advertising.

You can manage cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking third-party cookies or storage may affect whether some embedded videos work correctly.

6. Search Data

The current KARAKU search feature processes the text you type locally in the browser to match titles, summaries, genres, categories, years, and ratings.

In the supplied website code, the search query is not submitted to a KARAKU backend or saved as a KARAKU account history.

This statement applies to the current implementation. If remote search, analytics, or server-side logging is later added, this policy should be updated accordingly.

7. Third-Party Video Services

KARAKU may embed or link to videos hosted by external services. Depending on the catalog configuration, these may include YouTube, Facebook, TikTok, Google Drive, or other video providers.

Information third parties may receive

When an embedded player or external video is loaded, your browser may communicate directly with the provider. The provider may receive information such as:

  • your IP address;
  • browser and device information;
  • operating system and language settings;
  • the page that loaded the embed;
  • video playback and interaction information;
  • cookie or account identifiers, where applicable; and
  • other technical information described in the provider’s own privacy policy.

YouTube

KARAKU’s current player uses YouTube’s youtube-nocookie.com embed domain for YouTube content. This is intended to use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced embed mode, but Google or YouTube may still process information when you interact with the player in accordance with their own policies.

Facebook

KARAKU may load public Facebook videos through Facebook’s official video plugin. Facebook may process information when its player is loaded or used.

TikTok and other providers

If TikTok, Google Drive, or another provider is used for a particular title or episode, that provider’s own privacy and cookie practices apply to the interaction.

8. Hosting and Technical Logs

Even when KARAKU does not directly ask for personal information, the company or service that hosts the website may automatically process standard technical information needed to deliver the site.

This can include IP addresses, request times, requested pages or files, browser type, device information, error logs, security information, and similar server or network data.

The exact information retained depends on the hosting provider and deployment configuration. The supplied index.html file alone does not identify those hosting settings.

9. How Information May Be Used

Information processed in connection with KARAKU may be used to:

  • provide and display the website and its content;
  • remember your selected theme;
  • operate search, navigation, video playback, and other interface functions;
  • maintain security and prevent misuse;
  • diagnose technical problems and maintain availability;
  • comply with legal obligations; and
  • protect the rights, property, or safety of KARAKU, Accxs Creations, users, or others.

10. Sharing and Disclosure

KARAKU does not currently include a front-end feature that sells personal information or asks users to submit personal information for sale.

Information may nevertheless be processed by or disclosed to third parties in the following circumstances:

  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: where necessary to deliver and secure the website.
  • Video and content providers: when their embedded players, links, or media are loaded.
  • Legal compliance: where disclosure is required by law, legal process, court order, or a valid government request.
  • Protection and security: where reasonably necessary to investigate abuse, protect users, enforce terms, or defend legal rights.
  • Business changes: if ownership or operation of KARAKU or Accxs Creations changes, subject to applicable law.

11. Data Retention

Your theme preference remains in your browser’s local storage until you clear it, your browser removes it, or the site changes or removes that setting.

Any logs maintained by a hosting provider or information processed by a third-party video provider are retained according to that provider’s systems, settings, and privacy policy.

KARAKU should retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer period is required or permitted by law.

12. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures should be used to protect the website and information processed through it. However, no website, browser storage system, network connection, or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for maintaining the security of your own device, browser, network connection, and any third-party accounts you use while interacting with embedded services.

13. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live and which laws apply, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to request access, correction, deletion, or other controls.

For the current KARAKU interface, you can also take the following direct actions:

  • clear the karaku-theme local storage value through your browser;
  • block or delete cookies and site data through your browser settings;
  • disable third-party cookies, subject to possible loss of embedded-player functionality;
  • avoid playing or opening third-party embedded videos if you do not want to connect to those providers; and
  • use privacy or tracking-protection features provided by your browser.

14. Children and Minors

KARAKU is a general entertainment interface and is not designed as a service for collecting personal information from children.

If you are a minor under the laws that apply to you, you should use online services with the involvement or permission of a parent or legal guardian where required.

Third-party video providers may have their own age requirements and parental-control policies.

15. International Processing

Third-party content providers, hosting companies, or infrastructure services may process information in countries other than the country where you are located.

Their handling of information is governed by their own legal obligations, privacy policies, and data-transfer practices.

16. External Links

KARAKU may contain links to third-party websites or original video pages. When you leave KARAKU or open an external provider, this Privacy Policy no longer controls what that third party does with information.

You should review the privacy policy and terms of any external service you choose to use.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when KARAKU adds or changes features, hosting, analytics, advertising, accounts, forms, video providers, cookies, storage practices, or legal requirements.

The current version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

18. Contact

KARAKU is operated under Accxs Creations. Questions about privacy, personal information, data requests, or this policy should be submitted through the official contact method published by KARAKU or Accxs Creations.

If you later add a dedicated privacy or support email address, update this section so users can submit privacy requests directly.
